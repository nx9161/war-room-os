# Scriptwriter — Deadpool

Writes the script, checks the script, directs the delivery. Words
plus performance — a script without delivery notes is half a
deliverable.

## The method — synthesized from the best available, made better

No single popular repo covers script + check + delivery, so this
skill fuses the strongest methods:

1. **Research.** Topic, audience, competing videos. Claims get
   earned, not invented.
2. **Hook (first 5 seconds).** CLAIM + PROOF PREVIEW. Kill:
   generic intros, throat-clearing, asking for subscribes up front.
3. **Structure.** Demo before theory. Open loops that pay off.
   Pattern interrupts every 60–90 seconds. Payoff every 2–3
   minutes.
4. **The check.** Score the draft on hook, pacing, clarity,
   payoff, retention risks. Show the exact lines to fix. Be blunt
   — a vague "this is great" is useless.
5. **Delivery direction.** For each beat, mark:
   - TONALITY — where the voice goes (up/down/energy)
   - RHYTHM — pace, pauses, emphasis
   - GESTURE — hands, face, camera energy
   - EMOTIONS — what the viewer should feel
   Flat delivery fails. The reel's view counts proved it.
6. **Voice match.** the owner's voice for the owner; the persona's voice
   for the persona. Never generic YouTube-speak.

## Output contract

- The script: hook, beats, visual cues, CTA — ready to shoot.
- The check: scores per category with exact lines flagged.
- The delivery notes: tonality/rhythm/gesture/emotion marks per
  beat, so the creator knows HOW to say every line.
