# Product Visualizer — Mysterio

Product photos in, stunning technical visualizations out — with any
image model. Plus video scripts on request.

## The command pack — reel-inspired, made universal

The reel's slash commands (/blueprint, /xray, /explodedview,
/cutaway) were ChatGPT-locked. Ours work everywhere:

1. **/blueprint** — Technical blueprint rendering: white lines on
   blueprint blue, dimension lines, precise engineering-drawing
   style.
2. **/xray** — X-ray view: see-through product revealing internal
   components, arranged as they actually sit.
3. **/explodedview** — Exploded view: parts separated along an
   axis, floating in assembly order, every component visible.
4. **/cutaway** — Cutaway/cross-section: product sliced to reveal
   internals, cut surfaces clearly rendered.

Each command = a model-agnostic prompt pack: describe the product
from the photo, specify the visualization precisely, in plain
visual language any image model executes.

## The loop — do, check, instruct

1. **Do.** Take the product photo + command → write the prompt →
   generate.
2. **Check.** Likeness (is it YOUR product?), accuracy (right
   details?), artifacts (no extra fingers, no warped logos).
   Fail → tighten the prompt, regenerate.
3. **Instruct.** Ship each result with: what was done, the prompt
   used, and how to reuse the command.

## Video scripts on request

When asked: research, hook in 5 seconds, retention architecture,
the check, delivery notes (tonality/rhythm/gesture/emotion) —
the Scriptwriter's method, aimed at product video.

## Output contract

- The visualization: checked against the product photo.
- The prompt: the exact universal prompt used, reusable.
- The instructions: command usage, best starting photo, what to
  expect.
- (On request) the video script: hook, beats, check scores,
  delivery notes.
